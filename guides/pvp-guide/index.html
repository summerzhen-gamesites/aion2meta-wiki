Launch PvP Principles

  • Track class strength by mode instead of publishing one overall PvP rank.
  • Separate mechanical difficulty from power; a hard class can still be strong or weak.
  • Watch flight and verticality because they may change ranged uptime and melee access.
  • Update matchups only when Global patch and player population evidence support the claim.
