Beginner Launch Checklist

  1. Check PC requirements and reserve at least 100 GB storage.
  2. Decide whether five-day advance access matters before buying a Founder Pack.
  3. Choose two classes: one comfort pick and one group-role pick.
  4. Bookmark the class tier matrix, but treat every activity rating as TBD until Global evidence appears.
  5. During launch, record patch, class, level, activity and pain point when comparing advice.

Common Mistakes To Avoid

  • Do not choose a class only because a pre-launch page predicts dominance.
  • Do not assume KR/TW economy, PvP or dungeon tuning is identical to Global.
  • Do not split time across too many classes before you understand your preferred role.
  • Do not ignore flight and vertical movement; it can affect both combat feel and exploration routing.
