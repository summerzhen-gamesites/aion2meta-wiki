Four-Step Class Decision

  1. Choose range: decide whether you want to stay at range or commit near the frontline.
  2. Choose responsibility: tanking and healing are role choices, not merely shortcuts to party demand.
  3. Choose complexity: use the class pages' Beginner Complexity label as a learning-cost guide, not a power rank.
  4. Test before investing: confirm the movement, targeting and core loop feel right before committing scarce resources.

What This Guide Does Not Claim

Launch-week popularity, queue composition and regional rankings do not prove a class is best. Use the activity-specific tier pages only when their entries show a Global patch, test date and evidence.
