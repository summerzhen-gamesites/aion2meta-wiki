PvP Modes To Track Separately

1v1Duel and isolated matchup strength.
Small-scalePick pressure, utility and coordinated skirmish value.
Large-scaleGroup durability, ranged pressure, healing and area control.
Open-worldMobility, escape tools, terrain and flight behavior.

Before Entering PvP

  1. Put escape, defensive, control-break and recovery actions where they can be reached immediately.
  2. Know which teammate provides frontline pressure, healing, support or ranged damage.
  3. Choose a retreat direction before committing; flight and terrain can change pursuit and line of sight.
  4. Enter with an activity-specific setup once the relevant build is Global Verified.

Fight Priorities

  • Survive the opening: do not spend every defensive tool on the first threat.
  • Track control: distinguish a safe damage window from a target that can immediately counter or escape.
  • Focus with the group: coordinated pressure is more useful than isolated damage in team modes.
  • Protect the objective: a kill that abandons positioning or support can lose the larger fight.
  • Review by mode: a duel result does not establish small- or large-scale class strength.

How PvP Claims Are Ranked

The PvP tier list separates 1v1, small-scale and large-scale evidence. A matchup is not promoted to a Global recommendation without a patch, date, mode and repeatable reason.
