AION 2 Leveling Guide

Follow a low-risk progression loop: advance the main path, clear required unlocks, learn your class, and use repeatable content only when it solves a real progression block.

Leveling Priority

1. Main PathUse the main story and required objectives as the default route because they introduce progression systems and access.
2. Nearby UnlocksComplete tutorials and required side objectives that open travel, combat or account systems.
3. Power CheckIf progress slows, review equipment, skill use and survival before grinding unrelated activities.
4. Group ContentUse available dungeons or party activities when they provide a relevant objective, upgrade opportunity or practice.

When Progress Stalls

  1. Confirm the current objective and map marker rather than assuming more experience is required.
  2. Equip upgrades already earned and repair or replenish ordinary supplies if the client requires it.
  3. Review whether a tutorial, prerequisite or interaction was missed.
  4. Check known issues when an object or quest step does not respond.
  5. Only add repeatable content after identifying whether the blocker is level, gear, access or execution.

Route Accuracy

This guide does not publish an exact hour-by-hour route without a repeatable Global test. Quest rewards, dungeon unlock points and efficient detours should be versioned before they are presented as fastest.
