First-Session Checklist

  1. Check server status before troubleshooting a login problem locally.
  2. Choose the same region, server and faction as friends before investing in a character.
  3. Complete the opening path and tutorials instead of skipping systems you have not learned.
  4. Put frequently used combat, movement, healing and interaction actions on comfortable keys.
  5. Equip clear upgrades, but keep scarce currencies and materials until their purpose is understood.
  6. Try the class's normal combat loop before deciding it is weak or spending heavily on it.

A Safe Progression Loop

AdvanceFollow the main path until a system, area or activity opens.
LearnRead the unlock prompt and practice the new action before moving on.
UpgradeUse obvious equipment improvements; delay irreversible or expensive optimization.
GroupEnter party content after understanding your role and basic survival tools.

Common Mistakes To Avoid

  • Do not choose a class only because a launch-week page predicts dominance.
  • Do not assume KR/TW economy, PvP or dungeon tuning is identical to Global.
  • Do not spread limited resources across many characters before choosing a main.
  • Do not ignore flight and vertical movement; learn where it changes navigation or combat positioning.
  • Do not use unofficial account-linking workarounds while the official issue remains under investigation.
