Before Spending A Scarce Resource

  1. Identify whether the item is temporary or part of a longer progression path.
  2. Confirm the upgrade applies to the activity and role you actually play.
  3. Check whether enhancement, transfer or replacement behavior is documented in the Global client.
  4. Keep a reserve until the cost of the next progression step is known.

Evidence Limits

Exact stat weights, best-in-slot lists, enhancement breakpoints, crafting costs and market prices are intentionally omitted until Global data is reproducible. Use the class build pages for versioned recommendations as they are verified.
