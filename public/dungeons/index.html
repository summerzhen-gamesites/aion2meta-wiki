Why This Hub Exists Now

GSC already shows early dungeon demand, but individual dungeon pages should wait for verified Global names and mechanics. This hub captures the demand without inventing dozens of thin URLs before the test produces real data.

Launch Verification Priorities

  • Record the first dungeon names exactly as they appear in the Global client.
  • Separate solo, 5-player and 10-player content instead of merging them into one list.
  • Capture requirements, boss names, mechanics and reward screenshots before publishing an individual dungeon page.
  • Track which classes feel valuable by activity, but avoid turning one dungeon impression into a site-wide tier claim.
