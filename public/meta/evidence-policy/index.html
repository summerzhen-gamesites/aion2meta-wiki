Ranking Rules

  1. No class receives a final rank without patch, date, evidence type and reason.
  2. PvE, solo, 1v1, small-scale PvP and large-scale PvP are tracked separately.
  3. KR/TW information can influence what to test, but cannot be presented as Global proof.
  4. Every changed rating should create an update-log entry.
