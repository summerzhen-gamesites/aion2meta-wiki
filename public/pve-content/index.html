10-Player ContentPrioritize coordination, mechanic assignments and group survival over personal damage claims.
Repeatable GoalsTarget documented upgrades, event objectives or rankings with the current patch and reset schedule recorded.

Before Joining A Dungeon

  1. Confirm the activity's current entry requirement and whether your role is needed.
  2. Equip sensible upgrades and place survival or support tools on accessible keys.
  3. Read verified mechanics on the dungeon hub; do not rely on an unlabeled regional guide.
  4. After a run, identify whether the blocker was mechanics, coordination, survival or damage before changing the build.
