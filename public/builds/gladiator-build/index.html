Quick Start

  1. Position: begin where the melee role can perform without spending its escape.
  2. Setup: establish the resource, control, buff, debuff or target condition the class needs.
  3. Commit: use the main pressure window while the target and encounter allow it.
  4. Recover: switch to safe repeatable actions while key tools return.
  5. Reset: reposition before starting the next sequence.

Gladiator decision loop: Approach safely → establish pressure → spend the damage window → defend through retaliation → re-engage.

Build Modes

PvE variation

Stay on target, preserve mobility for mechanics and align heavy attacks with safe damage windows.

  • Favor reliable uptime and encounter tools.
  • Keep one answer for movement, control or incoming damage.
  • Measure party contribution in the content format being tested.

PvP variation

Apply frontline pressure without chasing through control; save mobility or defense for the counter-engage.

  • Build for a named format: 1v1, small-scale or large-scale.
  • Complete engage, survival and disengage plans before adding damage.
  • Test several matchups before calling a result solved.

Solo variation

Pull deliberately, group only what you can control and keep one recovery option available.

  • Reduce downtime and keep a recovery plan for mistakes or adds.
  • Prefer a repeatable sequence over a perfect party burst window.
  • Reassess defense when moving from ordinary enemies to elites.

Stats, Gear And Stigma

Priority method: Confirm weapon scaling and offensive breakpoints first; compare damage gain with the survival needed to maintain melee uptime.

  1. Meet confirmed accuracy, survival or resource requirements.
  2. Compare one stat change at a time on the same target and sequence.
  3. Prefer repeatable uptime over a larger tooltip that rarely lands.
  4. Choose modifiers to solve the actual problem: damage, control, sustain, mobility or party utility.
  5. Save patch, item level, target and sample size with every recommendation.

Global Verification Checklist

  • Capture exact Global names and tooltips for each recommended skill and modifier.
  • Verify damage uptime, survivability tradeoffs, group demand.
  • Measure cooldown, cost, cast or animation time and positional conditions.
  • Test PvE on the same encounter and PvP in the same format before comparison.
  • Use Global Verified only when another tester can repeat the result.
