What Is Usable Now

  • Quick Start: positioning, resource and cooldown decisions matching the class role.
  • Activity variations: what to emphasize in PvE, PvP and solo play without inventing a universal loadout.
  • Verification order: client facts to check before publishing skill, stigma, stat or gear claims.
  • Version safety: no untested numbers or copied KR/TW build is labeled Global best.

Before You Copy A Build

Confirm tested date, region, patch and activity. A raid setup can be poor for solo progression; a 1v1 setup can sacrifice the support or area pressure a large fight needs. Treat each build as a starting decision system until its exact loadout receives a Global Verified label.
