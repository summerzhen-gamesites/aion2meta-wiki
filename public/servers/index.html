Queues And Recommended Servers

Official notices advise players facing long queues to consider a recommended server. New-server and recommendation labels can change, so check the client before creating a character.

Server Transfers

Planned for October 14: transfers are scheduled to begin within the same faction and are expected to be free initially. Advanced Access characters may transfer only to other Advanced Access servers, not commercial-launch servers. This feature is not live yet and the rules may change.

Instanced content, including dungeons, supports cross-server play according to the official notice.
