All Class Guides

Melee bruiser

Gladiator

Durable weapon fighter for players who want direct pressure and forgiving uptime. Best fit: Choose Gladiator if you want to stay in the fight, pressure nearby targets and trade burst precision for a forgiving frontline rhythm.

Open

Tank

Templar

Shield-led frontline class for players who want group responsibility and defensive control. Best fit: Choose Templar if you enjoy leading pulls, protecting teammates and controlling where enemies face and move.

Open

Melee burst

Assassin

Fast, positional damage dealer for players who enjoy setup, timing and target selection. Best fit: Choose Assassin if you enjoy target selection, positional play and short commitment windows where timing matters most.

Open

Ranged physical

Ranger

Mobile ranged class for players who want spacing, kiting and steady pressure. Best fit: Choose Ranger if you want physical ranged pressure, active spacing and safe attack opportunities while moving.

Open

Support hybrid

Chanter

Buff-oriented support that can contribute damage while strengthening a group. Best fit: Choose Chanter if you want to improve a party through buffs and utility while still contributing direct damage.

Open

Healer

Cleric

Primary healing and recovery role for players who want strong group value. Best fit: Choose Cleric if you want primary responsibility for recovery, cleansing and keeping a group stable.

Open

Ranged magic DPS

Sorcerer

Elemental caster for players who prefer burst windows and ranged control. Best fit: Choose Sorcerer if you want ranged magical burst, deliberate cast windows and planned control.

Open

Summoner

Spiritmaster

Pet and control focused mage for players who like pressure through companions and utility. Best fit: Choose Spiritmaster if you enjoy managing a companion, sustained pressure and situational utility.
