Who Should Play This Class

Choose Spiritmaster if you enjoy managing a companion, sustained pressure and situational utility.

Likely strengths

Pet-and-utility identity; layered pressure; flexible answers for players willing to manage more systems.

Tradeoffs to accept

Pet behavior and target access affect consistency; more effects and timers increase decision load.

How To Play It

Dungeon / group

PvE

Keep the pet on a valid target, maintain valuable effects without waste and reserve utility for mechanics.

Matchup framework

PvP

Pressure through multiple sources, limit enemy options and protect the pet and your position during resets.

Progression

Solo

Let the pet control contact, pull deliberately and use sustained effects to keep moving.

Core decision loop

Set pet target → establish sustained effects → apply utility/control → refresh expiring effects → redirect or reset.

First Session Checklist

  1. Learn the range and movement needed for the class job: Summoner / utility mage.
  2. Identify one pressure sequence, one defensive response and one escape or reposition option.
  3. Test the loop on ordinary enemies before judging dungeon or PvP performance.
  4. Open the Spiritmaster build and choose the correct activity variation.
  5. Record Global patch and tested activity before treating performance as evidence.
